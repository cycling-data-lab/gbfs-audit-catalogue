\subsection{Human-validation protocol (pre-registered)}\label{secAnnotationV2}
To probe construct validity we re-examined a stratified sample of the
certified catalogue against external imagery, blind to the pipeline output and
blind to each other. The earlier four-parallel-question instrument collapsed on
two interpretive items (Cohen's $\kappa = 0.17$ and $0.13$); we therefore
replaced it with a \emph{one-question-at-a-time decision tree}, pre-registered
(frozen sample with its SHA-256, locked codebook, unmodified analysis script
timestamped before any label is recorded).

\paragraph{Instrument.} The first node classifies the \emph{physical
infrastructure} (not the transient presence of vehicles) into five types:
docks with fixed anchor hardware, a ground free-floating zone, scooters only,
a car-sharing bay, or nothing visible (plus an explicit \emph{indeterminate}
escape). A docked station then receives a raw dock count (Q2); any located
object receives two placement questions, geographic perimeter (Q3a) and network
proximity ($\leq 1$\,km from a sibling or inside the network hull, Q3b). Per-rule
ground truth is derived \emph{a posteriori} from the adjudicated answers, never
from a pipeline-referencing verdict: A1 iff car-sharing; A3 iff no physical
dock; A4 iff Q3b\,=\,no; A5 iff Q3a\,=\,no; A6 iff docked with feed
capacity\,$=0$; A2 iff the counted docks are inconsistent with the declared
capacity ($\pm 50\,\%$); A7 is evaluated at the system level only.

\paragraph{Sampling ($n=422$, seed 42).} The design separates two goals,
because the publication variance lives at the operator level, not the station
level. (i) A \emph{simple random sample} of $300$ stations drawn from the
$46{,}307$-station catalogue estimates catalogue-level prevalence
($\pm 5.7\,\%$ at $95\,\%$ on any proportion) and validates the abundant common
classes. (ii) \emph{Operator-balanced booster strata} (round-robin across
distinct systems) cover the rare or decisive classes a random sample cannot:
the two A4 discordant sets ($40+40$ stations, the decisive ablation), A1
($30$ stations spanning all $17$ French car-sharing systems), A5 and A2.
Car-sharing is excluded from the geospatial strata (it terminates at the type
node). A2/A5/A7 are mono-operator or near-exhaustive in the French corpus and
confirm parser fidelity for that operator, not the rule's universality.

\paragraph{Adjudication and analysis.} Disagreements are resolved by
deterministically re-applying the locked codebook; genuinely ambiguous cases
are marked \emph{indeterminate} and excluded from the coefficients (never
defaulted for or against the pipeline), and the full raw inter-rater confusion
matrix is released so any reviewer can recompute alternative bounds. Because the
strata have extreme class prevalence, $\kappa$ and Krippendorff's $\alpha$ are
deflated by the prevalence paradox; the pre-registered primary coefficient is
therefore \textbf{Gwet's AC1}~\cite{Gwet2008AC1}, reported with the raw
agreement $P_o$ and the modal-class share so the paradox is visible. Per-rule
precision/recall are reported with a cluster-robust $95\,\%$ interval
bootstrapped over systems (alongside the naive Wilson
interval~\cite{Wilson1927Interval}), since stations within an operator are not
independent. Robustness analyses (Street-View-staleness sensitivity, an
attention-drift test on per-station timing, and an intra-rater test--retest
round) are pre-specified. The frozen sample, codebook and analysis script are
released (\texttt{experiments/human\_validation/}).
